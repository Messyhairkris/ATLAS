HOW TO USE THE REVIEW DESK

This page is itself a review item. Everything below can be practised on it, and
whatever you do here is your feedback about the Desk - it reaches the agent the
same way every other decision does.

THE FOUR STEPS
 1. Pick an item on the left. Its file opens in this pane, with line numbers.
 2. Read it. "open the file" opens it in its own program if you want more room.
 3. Decide, with the big buttons below the note box:
      APPROVE   the work is right. The agent applies it: a commit is made, a file
                is copied into place, or a crew job is marked done. No note needed.
      REJECT    the work is wrong. Say why in the note. The agent fixes or drops it.
      QUESTION  you want an answer first. Ask it in the note. The item comes back
                marked [new], in orange, when the agent has answered or changed it.
      DEFER     not now. Say why. It stays on your list and is never sent.
    Anything but APPROVE needs a note of at least a few words.
 4. SEND hands every finished decision to the agent at once. In the pop-up,
    "copy the message" gives you a line to paste into the chat if you want it
    picked up right away; "close the Review Desk" closes everything.

POINTING AT EXACT LINES
 Select one or more lines in this pane, write your note, then press
 "attach my note to the selected lines". The agent sees exactly which lines you
 meant, even if the file changes around them. "clear the anchor" removes it.

WHAT THE MARKS ON THE LEFT MEAN
 [ok] approved    [no] rejected    [?] question    [...] deferred
 [!]  decided but missing its reason
 [new] changed since you last looked - read it again
 Approved and sent items leave the list. Tick "show what I have finished" to
 see them again.

WHILE THE DESK IS OPEN
 New items the agent queues appear by themselves ("N new item(s) arrived").
 The top-left status overlay shows how many items are waiting ("NEEDS YOU").

PRACTISE HERE - AND TELL US WHAT TO IMPROVE
 On this page, your decision means:
      APPROVE   this guide is clear; nothing to change.
      QUESTION  something is unclear - ask, and the answer comes back here as [new].
      REJECT    something is wrong or missing - say what.
 Try pointing at a line: select the line that confused you (or the feature you
 wish the Desk had), attach your note to it, press QUESTION or REJECT, then SEND.
 The agent reads it, revises the Desk or this guide, and the guide comes back
 marked [new] so you can check the change.

 "how to use the Desk" at the top always brings you back to this page.
